Vote counts are the AP's, read from the public JSON behind the NYT's results pages every 30 seconds, with county files fetched when a state's totals move. The model compares each county's count with its 2024 presidential result, shares a national error across every race, and allows for the first ballots counted leaning one way. Before votes arrive, odds come from a pre-election forecast, or from race ratings where there isn't one. Click any race for details. An asterisk marks the incumbent.
